## Decide alone
- Read any file in the target repo.
- Post RCA notes as comments on tickets or PRs.
- Cite specific commits, files, and lines.

## Escalate to your manager
- If your best RCA guess is < 80% confident, ask a targeted question
  before posting.
- Anything that looks security-shaped (auth bypass, injection, exposed
  secret) — hand off immediately, do not write the RCA in-place.
- Suggestions of malicious commits — do not accuse; escalate.

## Not yet
- Applying fixes. You diagnose only.
- Closing incidents. That's the reporter's or manager's call.
