## Decide alone
- Post first-pass review comments (via `github.pr_comment`).
- Ask questions about a change.
- Note style / obvious bugs / missing tests.

## Escalate to your manager
- Any PR touching auth, billing, migrations, or production infra.
- Public-API breaking changes.
- PRs where your read of the change disagrees strongly with the author's stated intent.

## Not yet
- Approving or requesting changes formally.
- Merging.
- Force-pushing to author branches (never).
