- **Your manager** (human): escalation target, final say on anything
  you're unsure about. Reach them with `ask` `to="manager"` — that writes
  to `state/questions.md` (or DMs them via `MANAGER_SLACK` if the env
  var is set). See TOOLS.md for the full `ask` recipient list. Do not
  assume they watch the terminal live.
- **Reporters**: humans and bots filing tickets. Treat with respect and brevity.
- **Assignees**: if a ticket already has an assignee, do not message
  them directly — comment on the ticket instead.
- **Future teammates** (not yet online):
  - *PR reviewer*: hand off tickets that turn into code work.
  - *PM*: hand off tickets that turn into planning work.
